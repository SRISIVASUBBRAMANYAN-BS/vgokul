About Me

I am Gokul Vishwanathan, a dedicated and experienced professional with a strong foundation in civil engineering, academic leadership, and administration. With a rich blend of academic expertise and administrative proficiency, I have consistently demonstrated my commitment to fostering educational excellence and driving institutional growth.

I hold a Master of Engineering degree in Structural Engineering, awarded in May 2016. My academic journey began with a Bachelor of Engineering in Civil Engineering, completed in May 2014, and a Diploma in Civil Engineering, completed in May 2010. I am currently pursuing my PhD at SRM University, Tirupati.

Currently, I serve as the Assistant Manager, Administration at SRM Group of Institutions, Tirupati, a role I have held since November 2022. In this capacity, I collaborate with faculty, administrators, and students to facilitate educational programs, internships, and job placements, ensuring alignment with institutional goals. I am also actively involved in construction projects, coordinating with architects, engineers, and contractors to meet project timelines and budgets.

My role encompasses strategic planning, performance evaluation, and student engagement initiatives, which have significantly contributed to improving institutional performance and student outcomes. My diverse skills, unwavering dedication, and passion for fostering academic excellence position me as a valuable contributor to any professional setting.
